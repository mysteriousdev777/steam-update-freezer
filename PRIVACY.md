# Privacy & Analytics

This app uses strictly anonymous analytics (via [Aptabase](https://aptabase.com)) to help me
understand whether it's being used and working correctly.

**What I collect:**
Basic usage events — app launches, and manifest updates (game name, App ID, and the build you
pinned) — plus standard technical info like your OS, app version, and language.

**What I DO NOT collect:**
No IP addresses, Steam accounts, personal files, or any personally identifiable information (PII).
The data isn't tied to your identity, and only the official build sends anything at all.

*You can opt out anytime in Settings → Privacy & Analytics.*
